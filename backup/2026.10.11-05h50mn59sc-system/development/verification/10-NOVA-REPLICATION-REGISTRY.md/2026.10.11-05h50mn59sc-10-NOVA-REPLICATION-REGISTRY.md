# J. NOVA Replication Registry

| Methodology family | NOVA replication state | What NOVA can reproduce | What NOVA cannot legitimately claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standards mapping (NIST/OWASP/WCAG/CIS) | FULL | control matrices, checklists, evidence collection, coverage analysis | external certification/accreditation |
| Web automated scans | FULL | repeatable scans, normalized evidence, trend tracking | equivalence to human audit |
| Static/dependency analysis | FULL / PARTIAL | automated checks and evidence retention | proof of overall security |
| Formal verification | PARTIAL | properties, invariants, prover workflows, traceability | provider independence or completeness beyond the specification |
| Manual smart-contract audit | PARTIAL | pre-audit preparation, threat modeling, adversarial review workflow | independent assurance |
| Bug bounty | PARTIAL | policy, scope, severity model, triage workflow | external researcher reach or platform reputation |
| Audit contest | PARTIAL | contest-like internal review | independent crowd breadth/reputation |
| Exchange listing | NOT REPLICABLE | readiness dossier and evidence package | listing decision or proprietary ranking |
| Market-data methodology | PARTIAL | normalization, provenance, reconciliation, anomaly checks | proprietary upstream labels/data-quality claims |
| Regulatory review | NOT REPLICABLE | jurisdiction trigger matrix and evidence preparation | regulator's legal judgment/authorization |
| Reserve assurance | PARTIAL | on-chain reconciliation and evidence pipeline | independent accounting attestation |
| Peer review | PARTIAL | reproducible research package and reviewer checklist | journal/editorial authority |
| Media verification | NOT REPLICABLE | fact dossier and corrections process | editorial independence/publication decision |
| Community review | FULL workflow / PARTIAL trust | issue intake, moderation, contribution history | independently trustworthy community reputation |
| Threat-intelligence monitoring | PARTIAL | watchlists, feeds, alerts, correlation | proprietary attribution/privileged intelligence |

## Core boundary
NOVA can reproduce methods and preparation workflows where evidence and mechanics are public. It cannot manufacture the independence or authority that is itself part of an external verification mechanism.
