# I. Adoption Registry

## Direct adoption effects
- Listings/discovery can reduce discovery friction for users.
- Security assurance can reduce counterparty uncertainty.
- Regulatory readiness can remove legal-entry barriers where applicable.
- Research validation can increase intellectual credibility and discoverability.
- Community processes can improve contributor participation and accountability.
- Repository transparency can support developer adoption and external review.

## Evidence limitation
Stars, followers, Discord membership, social engagement and similar public metrics are adoption signals, not independent evidence of product quality, security, legality or trustworthiness.

## Measurement rule
Treat adoption impact as a separate dimension from visibility and credibility. Do not infer causal adoption effects without evidence.
