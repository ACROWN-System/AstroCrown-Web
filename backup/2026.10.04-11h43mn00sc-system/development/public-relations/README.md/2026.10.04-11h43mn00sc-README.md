# AstroCrown Public Relations

## Purpose

This subsystem is the active repository home for **Public Relations** research concerning AstroCrown and the Space Empire.

It answers a different question from `development/verification/`:

> Who matters to AstroCrown's visibility, reputation, relationships, capital access, strategic introductions, media attention, ecosystem adoption, partnerships, listings, research exposure and public narrative — and which people inside those organizations can actually make or influence those decisions?

## Terminology rule: Public Relations vs GitHub pull request

Inside this repository, **Public Relations** is written in full or as **public-relations**.

The abbreviation **PR is not used as the repository's primary term for Public Relations** because GitHub conventionally uses “PR” for **Pull Request**.

Use:
- **Public Relations**
- **public-relations**
- **Public Relations target**
- **Public Relations research**
- **Public Relations outreach**
- **GitHub pull request**

Avoid ambiguous repository language such as “PR target”, “PR branch” or “PR status”.

## Boundary with verification

`development/verification/` remains responsible for external verification, audit, standards, assurance, benchmarking, regulatory and trust mechanisms.

This subsystem may use verification findings as evidence, but its target selection is driven by **strategic Public Relations relevance**, not by completeness of the verification ecosystem.

## Research principles

1. Do not equate visibility with authority.
2. Do not equate follower count with strategic value.
3. Do not assume the CEO is the actual decision-maker.
4. Identify the operational decision chain inside each organization.
5. Separate verified identities from observed, candidate and unknown identities.
6. Preserve historical relationships and account-state changes.
7. Treat X as a discovery and communication channel, not as sole legal, regulatory, security, investment or contractual authority.
8. Prefer relevance to breadth.
9. Search for people who can **decide, recommend, introduce, fund, integrate, list, report, research or amplify** AstroCrown or the Space Empire.
10. Re-verify current roles and handles before consequential interaction.

## Scope

The active universe should primarily cover:

- capital and strategic investors;
- exchange, listing and liquidity decision chains;
- strategic infrastructure and ecosystem partners;
- Space Empire / space-economy networks;
- AI / NOVA / frontier-technology networks;
- media and editorial decision chains;
- high-signal researchers and amplifiers;
- India / Asia strategic networks;
- events, conferences and community gateways;
- security/trust relationships where public visibility or institutional confidence is relevant;
- new people discovered through relationship graphs.

## Evidence model

Each target should ultimately be recorded as:

**organization → person/team → current role → decision function → X account → evidence → relationship state → AstroCrown relevance → next research action**

A missing X handle means **not established**, not “does not exist”.

A candidate target must remain a candidate until evidence supports a stronger state.
