# D. Necessity Registry

## Trigger-dependent regulatory necessity
- SEC: applicable only where the actual asset/activity/perimeter invokes relevant U.S. securities-law requirements.
- ESMA/MiCA: applicable where the relevant EU offer, admission, CASP or other MiCA perimeter applies.
- FCA: applicable to in-scope UK cryptoasset activities; current and future UK regimes must be mapped separately.
- FIU-IND: applicable when AstroCrown/entity activity falls inside India's VDA-service-provider AML/CFT perimeter.
- MAS / SEBI / other regulators: activity- and jurisdiction-dependent.
- PCI DSS: only where card-data/payment-card environments are actually in scope.
- ISO 27001 / ISO 27701 / SOC 2: not universal prerequisites; strategically or contractually important when counterparties require them.

## Strongly recommended engineering assurance
- SSDF / ASVS evidence discipline.
- WCAG plus accessibility checks.
- Lighthouse / PageSpeed / CrUX.
- repository change controls and reproducible evidence.
- independent smart-contract assurance before material production exposure where applicable.
- formal verification where critical properties justify it.
- bug bounty/adversarial disclosure after a meaningful public attack surface exists.

## Opportunity-driven
- CoinGecko / CoinMarketCap / DefiLlama listings.
- CEX applications.
- major media coverage.
- academic publication and peer review.
- visibility analytics.

Opportunity-driven does not mean low-value; it means not intrinsically a prerequisite to building the system.
