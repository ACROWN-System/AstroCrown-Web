# G. Visibility Registry

## Extreme / high visibility mechanisms
- CoinGecko / CoinMarketCap public listings and market data.
- Major exchange listings.
- Major editorial/media coverage.
- GitHub public development activity.
- Major public bug-bounty/security records.
- Public regulatory registers where applicable.
- Public research/publication and citation pathways.

## Visibility dimensions
Record separately:
- direct publication;
- algorithmic ranking;
- search/discovery exposure;
- editorial coverage;
- community amplification;
- institutional reference.

Visibility is not equivalent to credibility, and a public ranking is not a security or legal assurance.

## Data quality
Every visibility observation should retain date, source, scope/population where relevant, methodology and limitations.
