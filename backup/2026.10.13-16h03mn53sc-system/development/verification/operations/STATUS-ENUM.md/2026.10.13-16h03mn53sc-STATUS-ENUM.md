# Verification Status Vocabulary

## Evidence states
- VERIFIED — directly supported by inspected repository material or authoritative/current external evidence.
- DERIVED — AstroCrown-specific interpretation logically derived from verified evidence.
- CANDIDATE — relevant but not yet sufficiently researched/approved.
- UNKNOWN — evidence is insufficient.
- BLOCKED — investigation/verification is prevented by a defined missing dependency.

## Operational statuses
- Not Investigated
- Researching
- Candidate
- Planned
- In Progress
- Submitted
- Passed
- Failed
- Expired
- Superseded
- Blocked

Evidence state and operational status are separate dimensions.

## Mandatory controls
- Do not invent costs.
- Do not convert unknown into negative facts.
- Do not convert blocked into pass.
- Do not treat rankings as verification.
- Preserve source disagreement.
- Preserve date/version/commit scope.
