# AstroCrown + Space Empire — Strategic Public Relations Target Universe
## 2026-10-03

## Purpose

This is the **active** X/Public Relations research universe for AstroCrown and the Space Empire.

The objective is not to collect the largest possible number of accounts. The objective is to identify the people and organizations most capable of:

- discovering AstroCrown;
- understanding its architecture and long-term purpose;
- providing introductions;
- allocating capital;
- selecting or recommending an investment;
- enabling listings or liquidity;
- establishing strategic partnerships;
- integrating infrastructure;
- producing credible research;
- choosing media coverage;
- inviting AstroCrown into relevant events;
- amplifying the project to the right audience;
- opening Space Empire relationships;
- connecting AstroCrown with AI, infrastructure, finance, payments, Web3 and space ecosystems.

This is a research universe, not a claim that a target will invest in, endorse, list, partner with or promote AstroCrown.

# 12. The decision-chain rule

For every promising organization, future X research should continue until the following chain is reasonably understood:

**public account → leadership → functional team → actual decision-maker → recommender/influencer → relevant gateway**

Examples:

**Investment**
CEO/founder → general partner → partner → principal → scout/analyst

**Exchange**
CEO → listing leadership → asset-review/listings → ecosystem/BD → regional contact

**Media**
publication → editor → reporter → producer → audience/social

**Influencer**
creator → manager/business lead → producer/researcher

**Space**
company → program leadership → partnerships/BD → mission/program team → ecosystem gateway

**AI**
company → research/product leadership → partnerships → developer/community → relevant researcher

# 17. Security and authenticity

X must never be treated as sole authority for:

- legal identity;
- investment commitment;
- exchange listing;
- regulatory interpretation;
- audit/security conclusions;
- custody;
- contract execution;
- financial credentials.

Before consequential interaction:

**official website → current organizational identity → person/role cross-check → purpose-specific channel → non-sensitive communication**

Never send private keys, seed phrases, authentication secrets, API credentials or confidential financial material through X.

A high follower count does not establish authority.

A checkmark does not establish organizational authorization.
