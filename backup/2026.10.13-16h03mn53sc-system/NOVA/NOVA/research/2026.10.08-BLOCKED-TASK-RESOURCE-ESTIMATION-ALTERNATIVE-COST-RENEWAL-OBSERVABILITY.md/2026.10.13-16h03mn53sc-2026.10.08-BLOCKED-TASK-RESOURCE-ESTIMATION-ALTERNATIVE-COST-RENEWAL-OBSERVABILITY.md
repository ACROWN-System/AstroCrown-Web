# NOVA task-level resource economics — blocked dependency

Date: 2026-10-08
Status: BLOCKED / RESEARCH-READY

## Why this remains blocked

The provider-level allocator can now observe expiration/reset opportunities, renewal characteristics, protected reserve, and economic metadata when explicitly supplied. It must not autonomously consume or prioritize scarce capacity without knowing whether the specific task produces enough value to justify the resource consumption and its side effects.

## Required dependencies

- TASK-RESOURCE-ESTIMATION-BENCHMARK — reliable estimates of requests, tokens, GPU seconds, memory, latency, and other resource units for a proposed task.
- ALTERNATIVE-RESOURCE-COST-FORECAST — comparable expected cost and availability of other eligible providers/resources at the same decision point.
- PROVIDER-RENEWAL-OBSERVABILITY — explicit or verified observations for renewal period, replenishment behavior, or expiration semantics where the provider exposes them.
- NEGATIVE-EFFECTS-MODEL — measurable penalties for rate-limit pressure, reduced reserve, latency impact, quality degradation, failure risk, or other downstream effects.

## Intended decision

For a task already required by the system, NOVA should evaluate something equivalent to:

expected task benefit − direct resource cost − renewal/opportunity cost − negative effects

and compare that result against available alternatives.

This is intentionally not implemented as a guessed universal formula. The weights and units must be benchmarked for the actual workload.

## Important boundary

Never generate work merely to consume capacity before expiration.

Never prefer paid capacity merely because its allocation is close to resetting.

Never treat a rapidly renewable quota as equally scarce to a slow or non-renewable allocation without evidence.

Never infer provider pricing, renewal behavior, or external economic impact from missing data.

## Re-entry condition

When the named dependencies are available, implement task-aware scheduling as an extension of the current capacity opportunity API rather than replacing the existing observation/freshness model.
