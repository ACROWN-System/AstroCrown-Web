# NOVA nonlinear interaction / physics-informed semantics — blocked dependencies

Date: 2026-10-08
Status: BLOCKED / RESEARCH-READY

This file deliberately records discoveries and boundaries without presenting unvalidated concepts as implemented capabilities.

## Discovery

The next computational layer should investigate a nonlinear interaction field rather than assuming graph propagation is the primary abstraction.

Candidate properties:

- higher-order interactions that are not reducible to independent pairwise edges
- time-dependent observations rather than permanent known states
- empirical exploitation of correlations without requiring causal explanation
- sparse candidate generation before expensive evaluation
- GPU execution where parallel tensor/vector operations materially reduce latency, cost, or energy
- measurement/commit as a distinct operation from possibility/candidate generation
- explicit handling of uncertainty, staleness, contradiction, and observation freshness

## Not implemented yet

### 1. Physics-informed semantic operators

Potential mappings inspired by experimentally established physics include superposition/alternative candidates, interference-like reinforcement/suppression, entanglement-like non-separable relationships, decoherence-like loss of useful coherence, and measurement/collapse semantics.

These are not implementation claims. Each mapping requires a formal software definition and tests demonstrating that the abstraction provides measurable utility beyond existing mechanisms.

Dependency: FULL-SOURCE-TRANSCRIPT — the currently attached transcription only contained section headings, so the video-specific scientific claims cannot yet be treated as source-established requirements.

### 2. Nonlinear interaction field engine

A classical engine capable of evaluating higher-order interactions while preserving temporal context and uncertainty is not yet implemented.

Dependency: GPU-COMPUTE-ACCESS + GPU-BENCHMARK-HARNESS — implementation should be selected only after measuring candidate approaches under actual free/available GPU constraints.

### 3. Efficient search without brute-force combinatorics

N-way interaction spaces can grow too quickly for naive enumeration.

Dependency: BENCHMARKED-SPARSE-SEARCH — candidate generation, pruning, approximate methods, sparse tensors, attention-like mechanisms, kernels, or other strategies must compete on measured speed/cost/quality rather than being selected by assumption.

### 4. Empirical usefulness criteria

The engine does not need to explain why a relationship exists in order to exploit a relationship that repeatedly improves outcomes.

However, it must measure whether the relationship is useful, stable enough for its operating conditions, and cheaper than the baseline.

Dependency: REPRODUCIBLE-EMPIRICAL-EVALUATION — requires datasets/workloads, baselines, metrics, and repeated trials.

### 5. Reality/state separation

Already partially implemented through freshness-aware observations.

Not yet complete for every NOVA subsystem.

Dependency: SEMANTIC-STATE-INVENTORY — every subsystem that currently stores a derived state must be reviewed to determine whether it is an observation, interpretation, prediction, policy result, or committed action.

## Architectural decision recorded here

Do not use KNOWN STATES as a foundational primitive.

Use observations and derived assertions with explicit provenance and temporal validity instead.

Do not require causal explainability as a prerequisite for operational use.

Do not commit to graph propagation, hypergraphs, Transformers, tensor networks, or any other established architecture before benchmark evidence shows that it is the best fit for the actual workload.

## Re-entry conditions

This work becomes implementable when the named dependencies are available or sufficiently characterized. The resulting implementation should begin with a classical, hardware-independent semantic contract and use GPU acceleration as an optimization layer, not as a hidden semantic requirement.
