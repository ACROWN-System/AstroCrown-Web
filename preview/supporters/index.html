ACROWN Genesis Programme

Take part in AstroCrown's beginning.
A separate future ACROWN purchase allowance—not free tokens.

The ACROWN Genesis Programme is for eligible Genesis Programme Participants who make a verified contribution during its initial phase. Each USDT 1 of verified contribution creates a wallet-specific allowance to make a separate future purchase of up to 25 ACROWN at the applicable ACS floor. The contribution is not a token purchase, does not pay the later purchase consideration and does not automatically enter the ACS reserve. The full floor-price consideration must reach reserve V. When Paymaster is active, one all-inclusive ACROWN-denominated fee includes network execution; otherwise, the wallet pays native network gas directly.

What the multiplier means

The approved rule is 25 ACROWN of purchase allowance for each 1 USD₮ of verified contribution. That contribution does not pay the purchase price. The full ACS floor consideration must reach V. If Paymaster is active, its single ACROWN-denominated fee already includes network execution costs; there is no second network-fee charge. USDT 1,000,000 can create at most 25,000,000 ACROWN of aggregate purchase allowance.

Purchase period

There is no fixed time-based expiry. The programme follows its approved lifecycle rules until the shared 25,000,000-ACROWN sale capacity is exhausted or a separate owner-approved closure is published.

ACS protection

The ACS floor is F = V/C and may never decrease. The full floor consideration is credited to V. An active Paymaster displays one all-inclusive fee in ACROWN, including network execution cost; it is not duplicated as another gas line. A floor-price sale preserves F, all else equal.

Programme principles

What the ACROWN Genesis Programme does — and does not do

Contribution is not a token purchase

USDT contributed to the programme does not itself purchase ACROWN, enter circulation or automatically increase the ACS reserve. Any later token purchase is a separate transaction.

The multiplier is purchase eligibility, not free tokens

A verified qualifying contribution creates a personal ACROWN purchase allowance at 25 ACROWN per USDT 1. This is not an airdrop or bonus token issuance. The participant may purchase up to their unused personal allowance at the applicable ACS floor, subject to the fixed 25,000,000-ACROWN programme-wide ceiling and remaining capacity.

Used entitlement is not replenished by resale

Under the proposed model, each completed purchase consumes the corresponding part of the entitlement. Reselling those purchased tokens does not restore the consumed capacity.

Allocation and sale are different

A reserved purchase opportunity is not a completed sale. Unsold tokens remain platform inventory and do not enter circulating supply merely because an entitlement exists.

The ACS floor is protected from fees

The governing formula is F = V/C, where V is the reserve vault and C is circulating ACROWN. A purchase of q ACROWN must credit the full q × F to V. When Paymaster is active, one all-inclusive ACROWN-denominated fee includes network execution cost; no separate network-fee line is added. External market prices may differ from F.

ACS and Paymaster apply protocol economics

AstroCrown is designed to apply the same floor/roof and Paymaster economics to circular or self-directed trading as to every other transaction, rather than policing a holder's trading intent. The intended outcome is that round-trip costs make extraction unattractive and may benefit the community through the published fee design; this must be proven from the final mechanics, not assumed.

The qualification threshold and purchase cap

The multiplier is fixed at 25 ACROWN per USDT 1 of verified contribution, with an aggregate contribution ceiling of USDT 1,000,000 and a maximum programme-wide purchase allowance of 25,000,000 ACROWN. The minimum first contribution, floor-price calculation/version, rounding rules, programme duration/closure terms and applicable cancellation/refund terms must be published before contributions open. Each wallet's personal allowance is calculated only from verified contributions sent by that same wallet; the shared global cap is an additional limit.

Public accountability

Every use of Genesis Programme funds, publicly accounted for

participants and the public should be able to follow contributions into the programme treasury and trace every subsequent use of those funds.

The programme should use a dedicated, separately accounted-for Genesis Programme treasury. Its funds must remain distinguishable from the ACS reserve and the separately designated competition-prize allocation. The public record is intended to cover every confirmed contribution, outgoing payment, treasury-to-treasury transfer, network fee and off-chain expenditure attributable to the programme. Public accounting entries will be paired with independently checkable evidence wherever the underlying activity permits it.

1. Segregated Genesis Programme treasury

Genesis Programme funds will be separately accounted for and kept distinguishable from the ACS reserve and competition-prize allocation. Any transfer to another wallet or account must remain traceable.

2. Public contribution record

Each confirmed USDT contribution will be recorded with its date and time, network, token identifier, amount, receiving treasury address and transaction reference. The registered participant wallet, observed transaction source and account association will be stored as separate fields with an explicit verification status.

3. Every use of funds

Each outgoing transfer, internal treasury movement, payment, fee and off-chain expense will have a dated public ledger entry showing amount, purpose, destination or payee category, and supporting reference.

4. External verification

On-chain records will link directly to the relevant public block explorer. Off-chain expenditure will have supporting evidence such as an invoice, receipt or reconciled statement, with private data appropriately protected.

Questions

ACROWN Genesis Programme FAQ

Can I cancel a confirmed contribution or request a refund?

The programme is designed to be non-cancellable and to offer no voluntary refunds once a contribution has been confirmed and recorded. Review the final terms carefully before sending any funds. This policy does not remove a remedy or right that applicable law makes mandatory.

How can I verify what AstroCrown does with Genesis Programme funds?

The public ledger is intended to disclose every contribution and every use of programme funds. On-chain transactions will link to the correct network explorer; off-chain payments will be supported by accounting evidence such as receipts, invoices or reconciled records. An explorer link proves the on-chain movement, not by itself the purpose of the expenditure.

Can anyone purchase ACROWN before its official launch?

No public ACROWN Genesis Programme purchase is available before the official ACROWN launch, including for wallets that have already registered and earned an allowance. Contributions create only a future right to purchase; they do not buy, issue or deliver ACROWN. Specifically authorized administrator qualification tests are separate, auditable test activity and do not count as public participant purchases or change participant allowances.

Can an unused allowance expire?

An earned wallet-specific allowance does not expire merely because that wallet has not used it for a period of time under the current intended rules. The final programme duration and any programme-wide closure conditions remain to be confirmed and will be published before contributions open.

Does my USDT contribution buy ACROWN?

No. The proposed programme separates the Genesis Programme contribution from a later ACROWN purchase. The contribution does not itself issue tokens or automatically increase the ACS reserve. Any later purchase would be a separate transaction under final approved terms.

What does the multiplier allow me to do?

A verified qualifying contribution establishes a personal ACROWN purchase allowance at 25 ACROWN per USDT 1. Example: 1,000 USD₮ creates a right to purchase up to 25,000 ACROWN at the applicable ACS floor. This is permission to make a later purchase, not free tokens or automatic issuance. The programme-wide maximum is 25,000,000 ACROWN; the minimum first-contribution threshold and final floor-price/rounding rules must be published before launch.

Can I send USDT now?

No. This is a development preview and does not accept USDT. No receiving address or network is published. Do not send funds for this programme until an official, verified payment flow is enabled on the AstroCrown website.

How will Genesis Programme funds be used?

Contributions are received into dedicated programme treasuries and remain separately accounted for from ACS reserve V and the competition-prize allocation. The permitted uses, allocation rules and spending controls must be published before contributions open. No receiving addresses or payment function are available in this preview.

How does my participant display name and wallet registration work?

A random participant display name is generated when you register and you may customize it. Your connected wallet address is the public identifier that controls the entitlement and authorizes purchases. No legal name or identity document is required by the programme design. The public GitHub audit snapshot may link your pseudonym, wallet address and programme transactions. Never share your seed phrase or private key.

What does the USDT 1,000,000 contribution ceiling represent?

It is the maximum aggregate verified contribution across Ethereum, Tron and Solana. At the approved 25:1 multiplier, it can create no more than 25,000,000 ACROWN of programme purchase allowance. Each wallet's personal allowance is calculated separately from its own verified contributions; wallets linked to one participant do not pool balances. The shared purchase ceiling limits the aggregate ACROWN quantity all eligible wallets may buy at the approved floor price.

What happens if I send more USDT than the authorized amount?

Do not do this. Excess funds are not credited with extra entitlement and are not promised to be returned, refunded or compensated. A direct or excess transfer may be permanently unrecoverable. Even a failed or rejected blockchain transaction can still consume network fees. Never assume AstroCrown can recover or return excess funds.

Are gas and Paymaster fees included in the ACS floor price?

No. For a purchase of q ACROWN, the full q × F floor-price consideration must be credited to the ACS reserve V. When Paymaster is active, the user pays one all-inclusive Paymaster fee denominated in ACROWN; that fee already includes network execution cost and must not be duplicated as a separate network fee. Without Paymaster, native network gas is paid through the direct-wallet route. Neither route may reduce the floor-price credit to V.

Can I resell ACROWN after buying it?

Purchased ACROWN may be resold through an available external exchange or swap venue, subject to applicable terms and law. Resale does not restore the purchase allowance already consumed. A higher external price may create an opportunity only if it is executable after fees and slippage; the ACS floor does not guarantee an external market price, profit, listing or liquidity. Selling can also create market sell pressure. The native ACS floor/roof and Paymaster rules are intended to make circular activity costly to the trader and potentially value-accretive to the community under the approved fee allocation; AstroCrown does not police holder trading intent. This outcome requires formal economic proof and does not control third-party venues.

Does the ACS guarantee the external market price?

No. The ACS floor is an internally defined reserve-to-circulation measure, F = V/C, and the protocol must never allow it to decrease. A sale at F that credits the full consideration to V preserves the floor, all else equal. When Paymaster is active, its single ACROWN-denominated fee includes network execution cost; without Paymaster, direct native gas applies. The ACS floor is distinct from the price at which ACROWN might trade on an external venue.
