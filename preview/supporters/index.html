First production transition

A small, defined funding gate to leave the $0 environment

AstroCrown remains in its $0 qualification environment until the first paid production order is fully costed, independently checked and funded. The supporter programme is intended to cover that narrow transition, not an open-ended operating budget.

Proposed migration threshold
USDT 150

A provisional aggregate reserve target based on the current deployment-budget envelope, not a final provider quote or a live funding counter.

Initial hosting runway
3 months

The transition budget should cover at least the first three months of the selected production host, plus the domain and essential backup arrangements.

How the target could be reached
1 or 2

Illustratively, one USDT 150 contribution or two USDT 75 contributions would reach the aggregate target. These are examples, not individual contribution minimums.

What the reserve is meant to cover

Initial VPS/hosting prepayment, domain registration, essential independent backup, required network or conversion fees, minimum gas, migration and restore testing, and a modest contingency.

When the reserve can be spent

Only after the selected provider passes the required account, payment-network, terms, security and restore checks, and the actual order-level costs fit inside the cleared reserve.

What happens if the quote is higher

The transition does not begin. The budget and threshold must be revised transparently before contributions open; the programme must not quietly rely on an unfunded balance or a paid fallback.

Funding gate, not a promise of zero risk. Reaching USDT 150 would be a proposed minimum aggregate migration reserve, not proof that every provider or future operating cost is funded. It materially reduces the risk of starting migration without enough money for known initial costs, but it cannot eliminate vendor, security, availability, legal, renewal or future operating risks. Funds remain in the separate supporter treasury until the approved activation conditions are met.

The USDT 150 figure is provisional. Before contributions are enabled, the exact provider, accepted USDT network, domain price, backup plan, required fees and first three months of hosting must be verified from checkout/order quotes. If those costs plus contingency exceed USDT 150, the threshold must be increased before launch. Final terms must also explain how the segregated reserve is handled if the threshold is never reached, subject to mandatory legal rights.

Wallet and member profile

Your wallet is your membership and entitlement

The supporter controls the registered wallet. The wallet address is the public identifier that owns the multiplier entitlement and authorizes purchases; the member pseudonym is only a customizable display name.

1. Random member name by default

Registration generates a random pseudonym automatically. The supporter may customize it. No legal name, government ID, email identity or other legal-identity registration is required by this programme design.

2. The wallet controls the entitlement

The multiplier entitlement is attached to the registered wallet, not to the display name. The supporter uses that wallet to authorize the contribution and later ACROWN purchases. AstroCrown never asks for or stores the wallet's seed phrase or private key.

3. Public GitHub records and backups

The public audit record may show the wallet address, member pseudonym, verified contribution transaction, multiplier eligibility, completed purchases and remaining programme allowance. Dated, append-only snapshots should include checksums and reconciliation totals.

4. No identity-document collection

The programme identifies the entitlement through wallet control and a pseudonym. It does not claim to verify the legal identity of the wallet controller. The public wallet-to-pseudonym association is visible by design and must be explained before registration.

The wallet owner remains responsible for wallet security and transaction approvals. Never share a seed phrase, private key or recovery phrase with AstroCrown or anyone claiming to represent it. The repository is public, so only the expressly public wallet, pseudonym and audit fields described by the programme may be published; credentials and signing secrets must never be included.

Programme principles

What the supporter programme does — and does not do

Contribution is not a token purchase

USDT contributed to the programme does not itself purchase ACROWN, enter circulation or automatically increase the ACS reserve. Any later token purchase is a separate transaction.

The multiplier is purchase eligibility, not free tokens

The first qualifying contribution activates the approved multiplier entitlement for the registered wallet. It is not an airdrop or bonus token issuance. The wallet may purchase up to the entire remaining programme allowance at the applicable ACS floor, and all wallets share the same global ceiling.

Used entitlement is not replenished by resale

Under the proposed model, each completed purchase consumes the corresponding part of the entitlement. Reselling those purchased tokens does not restore the consumed capacity.

Allocation and sale are different

A reserved purchase opportunity is not a completed sale. Unsold tokens remain platform inventory and do not enter circulating supply merely because an entitlement exists.

The ACS floor is a protocol constraint

Programme operations must preserve the ACS-defined floor, F = V/C. External market prices may differ from the ACS floor and are not guaranteed by this page.

The first qualification threshold and global cap must be clear

The minimum first contribution, approved multiplier, total ACROWN programme allowance, purchase window and non-cancellable / no-voluntary-refund policy must be published before contributions open. There is no separate per-wallet token ceiling; purchases are limited by the shared programme balance.

Public accountability

Every use of supporter funds, publicly accounted for

Supporters and the public should be able to follow contributions into the programme treasury and trace every subsequent use of those funds.

The programme should use a dedicated, separately accounted-for supporter treasury. Its funds must remain distinguishable from the ACS reserve and the separately designated competition-prize allocation. The public record is intended to cover every confirmed contribution, outgoing payment, treasury-to-treasury transfer, network fee and off-chain expenditure attributable to the programme. Public accounting entries will be paired with independently checkable evidence wherever the underlying activity permits it.

1. Segregated supporter treasury

Supporter funds will be separately accounted for and kept distinguishable from the ACS reserve and competition-prize allocation. Any transfer to another wallet or account must remain traceable.

2. Public contribution record

Each confirmed USDT contribution will be recorded with its date and time, network, token identifier, amount, receiving treasury address and transaction reference. The registered supporter wallet, observed transaction source and account association will be stored as separate fields with an explicit verification status.

3. Every use of funds

Each outgoing transfer, internal treasury movement, payment, fee and off-chain expense will have a dated public ledger entry showing amount, purpose, destination or payee category, and supporting reference.

4. External verification

On-chain records will link directly to the relevant public block explorer. Off-chain expenditure will have supporting evidence such as an invoice, receipt or reconciled statement, with private data appropriately protected.

Questions

Supporter programme FAQ

Can I cancel a confirmed contribution or request a refund?

The programme is designed to be non-cancellable and to offer no voluntary refunds once a contribution has been confirmed and recorded. Review the final terms carefully before sending any funds. This policy does not remove a remedy or right that applicable law makes mandatory.

How can I verify what AstroCrown does with supporter funds?

The public ledger is intended to disclose every contribution and every use of programme funds. On-chain transactions will link to the correct network explorer; off-chain payments will be supported by accounting evidence such as receipts, invoices or reconciled records. An explorer link proves the on-chain movement, not by itself the purpose of the expenditure.

Does my USDT contribution buy ACROWN?

No. The proposed programme separates the supporter contribution from a later ACROWN purchase. The contribution does not itself issue tokens or automatically increase the ACS reserve. Any later purchase would be a separate transaction under final approved terms.

What does the multiplier allow me to do?

The first qualifying contribution activates the approved multiplier entitlement for the registered wallet. It does not create free tokens or a personal token quantity calculated as contribution × multiplier. An eligible wallet may purchase up to the entire remaining programme-wide ACROWN allowance at the applicable ACS floor. The final multiplier and first-contribution threshold must be published before launch.

Can I send USDT now?

No. This is a development preview and does not accept USDT. No receiving address or network is published. Do not send funds for this programme until an official, verified payment flow is enabled on the AstroCrown website.

What is the USDT 150 transition threshold?

It is a provisional aggregate funding gate intended to cover the initial move from $0 qualification to paid hosting, including at least three months of hosting, the domain, essential backup and required migration/network costs. It is not a provider quote. The final amount must be checked against actual order costs before contributions open; if those costs exceed USDT 150, the target must be raised.

How does my member name and wallet registration work?

A random member pseudonym is generated when you register and you may customize it. Your connected wallet address is the public identifier that controls the entitlement and authorizes purchases. No legal name or identity document is required by the programme design. The public GitHub audit snapshot may link your pseudonym, wallet address and programme transactions. Never share your seed phrase or private key.

Is the USDT 250,000 target the ACROWN purchase allowance?

No. USDT 250,000 is the provisional aggregate contribution planning ceiling. The maximum ACROWN quantity available for purchase is a separate programme-wide limit that must be approved and published independently. Every eligible wallet shares that same ACROWN allowance, and one wallet may purchase all of what remains.

What happens if I send more USDT than the authorized amount?

Do not do this. Excess funds are not credited with extra entitlement and are not promised to be returned, refunded or compensated. A direct or excess transfer may be permanently unrecoverable. Even a failed or rejected blockchain transaction can still consume network fees. Never assume AstroCrown can recover or return excess funds.

Does the ACS guarantee the external market price?

No. The ACS floor is an internally defined reserve-to-circulation measure, F = V/C. It is distinct from the price at which ACROWN might trade on an external venue.
