Genesis begins here.
A privileged path to ACROWN.

Genesis is the first funding stage for the complete AstroCrown website frontend and backend. A finalized qualifying payment establishes a wallet-bound, USDT-denominated right to make a later, separate ACROWN purchase at the qualified ACS pure floor. What the multiplier means

The proposed global tranches apply 30×, 25×, 20×, 15× and 10× to successive USDT 10,000 portions of finalized qualifying payments. The allowance is denominated in USDT purchase value and converts to an ACROWN quantity only when a later floor-price purchase is quoted.

Any ACROWN purchase or distribution must follow the ACS accounting rules. The official floor must not be reduced by programme operations.

Programme principles

What the Genesis Programme does — and does not do

Genesis funding and ACROWN purchase are separate

A Genesis qualifying payment funds the programme's planned launch of the complete AstroCrown website frontend and backend; it does not itself purchase or issue ACROWN and is not automatically counted in the ACS reserve. A later, separate ACROWN floor-price purchase sends its exact USDT principal to the approved ACS reserve destination counted in V.

The multiplier is purchase eligibility, not free tokens

A verified qualifying payment creates a personal ACROWN purchase entitlement under the approved multiplier and programme terms. This is not an airdrop or bonus token issuance. The primary wallet may purchase up to its unused entitlement at the applicable ACS floor, subject to any approved programme-wide cap.

Used entitlement is not replenished by resale

Under the proposed model, each completed purchase consumes the corresponding part of the entitlement. Reselling those purchased tokens does not restore the consumed capacity.

Allocation and sale are different

A reserved purchase opportunity is not a completed sale. Unsold tokens remain platform inventory and do not enter circulating supply merely because an entitlement exists.

The ACS floor is a protocol constraint

The pure floor is F = V/C. The full USDT principal paid for a later ACROWN floor-price purchase must reach the approved ACS reserve destination and be included in V without a participant network-fee deduction. The registered wallet owner pays the network fee separately. External market prices may differ from the ACS floor and are not guaranteed by this page.

Declining global tiers and aggregate allowance

The proposed global schedule is 30× for the first USDT 10,000 of cumulative qualifying payments, then 25×, 20×, 15× and 10× for each successive USDT 10,000 tranche. It produces up to USDT 1,000,000 of aggregate USDT-denominated purchase allowance from USDT 50,000 of qualifying payments. Boundary-crossing payments are split between tiers. Final activation requires published rules and technical qualification.

Public accountability

Every use of Genesis Programme funds, publicly accounted for

Genesis participants and the public should be able to follow qualifying payments into the programme treasury and trace every subsequent use of those funds.

Genesis qualifying payments are intended to fund the launch of the complete AstroCrown website frontend and backend. They must remain separately accounted for and distinguishable from the ACS reserve and the separately designated competition-prize allocation. A later ACROWN floor-price purchase is a different transaction: its full USDT principal is directed to the approved ACS reserve destination and counted in V, while the registered wallet owner pays the participant-side network fee separately. The public record is intended to cover every confirmed qualifying payment, outgoing payment, treasury-to-treasury transfer, network fee and off-chain expenditure attributable to the programme. Public accounting entries will be paired with independently checkable evidence wherever the underlying activity permits it. The detailed launch budget and reserve/operations segregation must be approved and published before activation.

1. Segregated wallet identity treasury

Genesis Programme funds will be separately accounted for and kept distinguishable from the ACS reserve and competition-prize allocation. Any transfer to another wallet or account must remain traceable.

2. Public qualifying payment record

Each confirmed USDT qualifying payment will be recorded with its date and time, network, token identifier, amount, receiving treasury address and transaction reference. The primary Genesis identity, pre-registered source address and its ownership-proof metadata will be recorded as distinct fields. A payment is accepted only when its on-chain sender matches an address already verified and linked to that primary identity for the same network.

3. Every use of funds

Each outgoing transfer, internal treasury movement, payment, fee and off-chain expense will have a dated public ledger entry showing amount, purpose, destination or payee category, and supporting reference.

4. External verification

On-chain records will link directly to the relevant public block explorer. Off-chain expenditure will have supporting evidence such as an invoice, receipt or reconciled statement, with private data appropriately protected.

Questions

Genesis Programme FAQ

Can I cancel a confirmed qualifying payment or request a refund?

The programme is designed to be non-cancellable and to offer no voluntary refunds once a qualifying payment has been confirmed and recorded. Review the final terms carefully before sending any funds. This policy does not remove a remedy or right that applicable law makes mandatory.

How can I verify how AstroCrown accounts for Genesis Programme funds?

The public ledger is intended to disclose every qualifying payment and every use of programme funds. On-chain transactions will link to the correct network explorer; off-chain payments will be supported by accounting evidence such as receipts, invoices or reconciled records. An explorer link proves the on-chain movement, not by itself the purpose of the expenditure.

Does my USDT qualifying payment buy ACROWN?

No. The Genesis Programme separates a qualifying payment from a later ACROWN purchase. The qualifying payment does not itself issue tokens or automatically increase the ACS reserve. Any later purchase would be a separate transaction under final approved terms.

What purchase allowance does the declining multiplier establish?

The proposed schedule applies 30× to the first USDT 10,000 of cumulative finalized qualifying payments; 25× to the next USDT 10,000; then 20×, 15× and 10× to each successive USDT 10,000 tranche. Payments crossing a boundary are split across the applicable tiers. USDT 10 in the first tier establishes up to USDT 300 of later purchase allowance; USDT 10 in the second establishes up to USDT 250. USDT 50,000 of qualifying payments creates up to USDT 1,000,000 aggregate USDT-denominated allowance. The allowance is a right to make a later, separate purchase up to that USDT value at the contemporaneous approved ACS pure floor; it is not free ACROWN, a fixed token quantity or a guaranteed return. The terms remain subject to final rules publication, inventory/ACS qualification and applicable legal review before payments open.

Who pays blockchain network fees, and are they deducted from my USDT amount?

The registered wallet owner pays the network fee separately, using the fee asset or network resources required by the selected chain. The exact USDT amount in the payment intent must arrive in full. No network fee is deducted from the qualifying payment, the later ACROWN purchase principal, or the amount counted in ACS reserve value V. The dashboard must show the exact USDT principal and destination separately from the wallet's fee prompt. Maintain sufficient network-fee balance and cancel if you do not accept the fee. Estimates can change. Do not use a route that deducts a withdrawal or service fee from the USDT principal.

Can I send USDT now?

No. This is a development preview. Ethereum, TRON and Solana payment acceptance is disabled. Do not send USDT or any other asset. No payment will be recognized until the official verified flow is enabled.

How does my Genesis display name and wallet identity work?

A random unique public display name is assigned after the primary wallet is verified. You can customize it only when it is not already used by another Genesis wallet; the service checks availability and reserves the name atomically. The primary wallet is the identity, recovery anchor and entitlement holder. If the primary wallet and its recovery capability are irretrievably lost, Genesis cannot restore the identity or entitlement. Explorer links reveal the full public address and its on-chain history. Never share your seed phrase or private key.

What happens if I send more USDT than the authorized amount?

Do not do this. Excess funds are not credited with extra entitlement and are not promised to be returned, refunded or compensated. A direct or excess transfer may be permanently unrecoverable. Even a failed or rejected blockchain transaction can still consume network fees. Never assume AstroCrown can recover or return excess funds.

Does the ACS guarantee the external market price?

No. The ACS floor is an internally defined reserve-to-circulation measure, F = V/C. It is distinct from the price at which ACROWN might trade on an external venue.
