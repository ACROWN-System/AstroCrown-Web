AstroCrown remains in its $0 qualification environment until the first paid production order is fully costed, independently checked and funded. The Genesis Programme is intended to cover that narrow transition, not an open-ended operating budget.

Initial hosting runway
3 months

The transition budget should cover at least the first three months of the selected production host, plus the domain and essential backup arrangements.

How the target could be reached
1 or 2

Illustratively, one USDT 150 qualifying payment or two USDT 75 qualifying payments would reach the aggregate target. These are examples, not individual qualifying payment minimums.

What the reserve is meant to cover

Initial VPS/hosting prepayment, domain wallet verification, essential independent backup, required network or conversion fees, minimum gas, migration and restore testing, and a modest contingency.

When the reserve can be spent

Only after the selected provider passes the required account, payment-network, terms, security and restore checks, and the actual order-level costs fit inside the cleared reserve.

What happens if the quote is higher

The transition does not begin. The budget and threshold must be revised transparently before qualifying payments open; the programme must not quietly rely on an unfunded balance or a paid fallback.

Funding gate, not a promise of zero risk. Reaching USDT 150 would be a proposed minimum aggregate migration reserve, not proof that every provider or future operating cost is funded. It materially reduces the risk of starting migration without enough money for known initial costs, but it cannot eliminate vendor, security, availability, legal, renewal or future operating risks. Funds remain in the separate wallet identity treasury until the approved activation conditions are met.

The USDT 150 figure is provisional. Before qualifying payments are enabled, the exact provider, accepted USDT network, domain price, backup plan, required fees and first three months of hosting must be verified from checkout/order quotes. If those costs plus contingency exceed USDT 150, the threshold must be increased before launch. Final terms must also explain how the segregated reserve is handled if the threshold is never reached, subject to mandatory legal rights.

The wallet owner remains responsible for wallet security and transaction approvals. Never share a seed phrase, private key or recovery phrase with AstroCrown or anyone claiming to represent it. The repository is public, so only the expressly public wallet, pseudonym and audit fields described by the programme may be published; credentials and signing secrets must never be included.

Programme principles

What the Genesis Programme does — and does not do

Qualifying payment is not a token purchase

A qualifying USDT payment does not itself purchase ACROWN, issue tokens, or automatically increase the ACS reserve. Any later token purchase is a separate transaction.

The multiplier is purchase eligibility, not free tokens

A verified qualifying payment creates a personal ACROWN purchase entitlement under the approved multiplier and programme terms. This is not an airdrop or bonus token issuance. The primary wallet may purchase up to its unused entitlement at the applicable ACS floor, subject to any approved programme-wide cap.

Used entitlement is not replenished by resale

Under the proposed model, each completed purchase consumes the corresponding part of the entitlement. Reselling those purchased tokens does not restore the consumed capacity.

Allocation and sale are different

A reserved purchase opportunity is not a completed sale. Unsold tokens remain platform inventory and do not enter circulating supply merely because an entitlement exists.

The ACS floor is a protocol constraint

Programme operations must preserve the ACS-defined floor, F = V/C. External market prices may differ from the ACS floor and are not guaranteed by this page.

The first qualification threshold and global cap must be clear

The minimum first qualifying payment, approved multiplier/rate, any programme-wide ACROWN cap, purchase window and applicable cancellation/refund terms must be published before qualifying payments open. Each primary wallet's personal allowance is calculated from verified qualifying payments and the approved multiplier; an approved global cap is an additional aggregate limit.

Public accountability

Every use of Genesis Programme funds, publicly accounted for

Genesis participants and the public should be able to follow qualifying payments into the programme treasury and trace every subsequent use of those funds.

The programme should use a dedicated, separately accounted-for wallet identity treasury. Its funds must remain distinguishable from the ACS reserve and the separately designated competition-prize allocation. The public record is intended to cover every confirmed qualifying payment, outgoing payment, treasury-to-treasury transfer, network fee and off-chain expenditure attributable to the programme. Public accounting entries will be paired with independently checkable evidence wherever the underlying activity permits it.

1. Segregated wallet identity treasury

Genesis Programme funds will be separately accounted for and kept distinguishable from the ACS reserve and competition-prize allocation. Any transfer to another wallet or account must remain traceable.

2. Public qualifying payment record

Each confirmed USDT qualifying payment will be recorded with its date and time, network, token identifier, amount, receiving treasury address and transaction reference. The wallet-verified wallet identity wallet, observed transaction source and account association will be stored as separate fields with an explicit verification status.

3. Every use of funds

Each outgoing transfer, internal treasury movement, payment, fee and off-chain expense will have a dated public ledger entry showing amount, purpose, destination or payee category, and supporting reference.

4. External verification

On-chain records will link directly to the relevant public block explorer. Off-chain expenditure will have supporting evidence such as an invoice, receipt or reconciled statement, with private data appropriately protected.

Questions

Genesis Programme FAQ

Can I cancel a confirmed qualifying payment or request a refund?

The programme is designed to be non-cancellable and to offer no voluntary refunds once a qualifying payment has been confirmed and recorded. Review the final terms carefully before sending any funds. This policy does not remove a remedy or right that applicable law makes mandatory.

How can I verify how AstroCrown accounts for Genesis Programme funds?

The public ledger is intended to disclose every qualifying payment and every use of programme funds. On-chain transactions will link to the correct network explorer; off-chain payments will be supported by accounting evidence such as receipts, invoices or reconciled records. An explorer link proves the on-chain movement, not by itself the purpose of the expenditure.

Does my USDT qualifying payment buy ACROWN?

No. The Genesis Programme separates a qualifying payment from a later ACROWN purchase. The qualifying payment does not itself issue tokens or automatically increase the ACS reserve. Any later purchase would be a separate transaction under final approved terms.

What does the multiplier allow me to do?

A verified qualifying payment establishes a personal ACROWN purchase entitlement using the approved multiplier/rate. Illustration only: 1,000 USD₮ at 25 ACROWN per USD₮ gives a 25,000 ACROWN allowance. This is permission to make a later purchase at the applicable ACS floor, not free tokens or automatic issuance. Any programme-wide cap also applies. The final rate, threshold, floor-price rules and cap must be published before launch.

Can I send USDT now?

No. This is a development preview. Ethereum, TRON and Solana payment acceptance is disabled. Do not send USDT or any other asset. No payment will be recognized until the official verified flow is enabled.

What is the USDT 150 transition threshold?

It is a provisional aggregate funding gate intended to cover the initial move from $0 qualification to paid hosting, including at least three months of hosting, the domain, essential backup and required migration/network costs. It is not a provider quote. The final amount must be checked against actual order costs before qualifying payments open; if those costs exceed USDT 150, the target must be raised.

How does my Genesis display name and wallet identity work?

A random unique public display name is assigned after the primary wallet is verified. You can customize it only when it is not already used by another Genesis wallet; the service checks availability and reserves the name atomically. The primary wallet is the identity, recovery anchor and entitlement holder. If the primary wallet and its recovery capability are irretrievably lost, Genesis cannot restore the identity or entitlement. Explorer links reveal the full public address and its on-chain history. Never share your seed phrase or private key.

Is the USDT 250,000 target the ACROWN purchase allowance?

No. USDT 250,000 is the provisional aggregate qualifying payment planning ceiling. A primary wallet's personal multiplier allowance is calculated separately from any programme-wide ACROWN cap. The programme-wide cap, if adopted, limits the aggregate purchase capacity of all Genesis Programme participants.

What happens if I send more USDT than the authorized amount?

Do not do this. Excess funds are not credited with extra entitlement and are not promised to be returned, refunded or compensated. A direct or excess transfer may be permanently unrecoverable. Even a failed or rejected blockchain transaction can still consume network fees. Never assume AstroCrown can recover or return excess funds.

Does the ACS guarantee the external market price?

No. The ACS floor is an internally defined reserve-to-circulation measure, F = V/C. It is distinct from the price at which ACROWN might trade on an external venue.
