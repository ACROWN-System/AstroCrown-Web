Qualifying-Payment Terms

AstroCrown is preparing the rules that will govern any future Genesis qualifying payment and the resulting USDT-denominated purchase allowance. The final document must be approved, versioned and published before any payment-intent request can be enabled.

1. One payment asset, three supported networks

The intended payment asset is USDT. The supported mainnet implementations are Ethereum (ERC-20), TRON (TRC-20) and Solana (SPL). These are network routes for the same USDT asset, not three different currencies. A payment is valid only when the exact canonical token, network, receiving address and amount specified by an approved payment intent are used.

2. Wallet identity and payment-address ownership

Each Genesis profile is controlled by one primary wallet. It is the only Genesis login and recovery anchor. A participant must separately prove ownership of every additional network address used to send a qualifying payment. A payment address does not become another login wallet or an alternative recovery method. Payments from unregistered addresses are not eligible for automatic qualification.

3. Payment evidence and qualification

A wallet popup, screenshot, balance display or public explorer page is not sufficient evidence of payment. AstroCrown's receiving-wallet monitor must independently verify the canonical USDT transfer, sender, destination, exact base-unit amount, inclusion and chain-specific finality against two distinct RPC node sources. A treasury receipt alone does not grant a Genesis Member status or purchase allowance. Qualification requires a matching, unexpired payment intent created for the same profile and verified source address before the transfer. When all three networks are caught up, qualifying receipts are settled behind the minimum independently agreed finalized/solidified chain-time watermark and in on-chain occurrence-time order. If multiple receipts have the same occurrence second, the deterministic tie-break order is monitor verification time, network, transaction hash and transfer/event/instruction index. The final tier is assigned at settlement, so the estimate displayed when requesting an intent is not a guaranteed final allowance.

4. Proposed global allowance schedule

The current engineering model uses five cumulative global USDT 10,000 principal tranches with proposed multipliers of 30×, 25×, 20×, 15× and 10×. The resulting proposed global principal target is USDT 50,000 and the proposed aggregate USDT-denominated purchase-allowance cap is USDT 1,000,000. These values are a proposed implementation baseline, not approved programme terms. The final terms must establish eligibility, minimum payment, rounding, the global receipt-order allocation method and deterministic tie-breaks, cancellation, expiry, underpayments/overpayments and the treatment of any remaining capacity. The final tier is calculated when a matching receipt is settled, not when an intent is created.

5. Network fees and exact transfer instructions

If payment acceptance is formally opened, the payment intent will display one exact USDT amount, one network, one canonical token identifier and one AstroCrown receiving address. The participant's network fee is paid separately using the network's native fee asset or resources; it is not deducted from the USDT principal. The website does not submit the payment transaction on the participant's behalf.

6. Purchase and ACROWN delivery are separate

A qualifying payment, if accepted under the final approved terms, creates a purchase allowance only. It does not automatically issue, mint or transfer ACROWN. Any later floor-price purchase, reserve accounting, ACROWN inventory allocation and Solana delivery require separately approved rules, custody controls and operational authorization.
