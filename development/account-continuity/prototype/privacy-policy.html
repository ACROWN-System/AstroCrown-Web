ASTROCROWN · ACCOUNT PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

DRAFT ONLY — NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION OR REAL-USER ACCEPTANCE

Draft revision: 0.4 · 28 November 2026

Status: This is a development draft for the wallet-only AstroCrown account and Genesis programme relationship. It must be reviewed against the actual production service operator, hosting configuration, data flows and applicable privacy law before real-user data is collected.

1. Which environment are you using?

The public Cloudflare staging website is a temporary test environment. Use a disposable wallet with no valuable assets and synthetic profile information. Staging account records, sessions, profiles and test data may be reset or deleted during qualification. Do not use a production wallet or send real funds.

A static HTML preview or local file preview is read-only and cannot create an account or authenticate a wallet. Account authentication is available only on the official HTTPS AstroCrown Worker staging origin marked for that purpose. Do not enter personal information or wallet recovery secrets into an untrusted preview.

This policy is a development draft and does not identify all production service providers, legal bases, jurisdictions, retention periods or data-subject request contacts. Those details must be completed before production onboarding.

2. The account identity model

Each ordinary AstroCrown account is identified by one primary EVM wallet public address. The primary wallet is the account sign-in identity and recovery anchor. Genesis profile information, public display name, programme membership, badge and entitlements are attached to the same AstroCrown account. Genesis is not a separate account or sign-in service.

The current primary sign-in flow supports EVM externally owned accounts using an EIP-191 personal-message signature. The browser discovers compatible wallet providers, the user chooses the provider and confirms the exact public address to use, and the Worker verifies a short-lived, origin-bound challenge before issuing a session.

AstroCrown never asks for, receives or stores a wallet private key, seed phrase, mnemonic or recovery words. Never enter them into the AstroCrown website or send them to AstroCrown staff, support personnel, NOVA or anyone else.

3. Information processed

The wallet-only account service may process and store the following information to provide the requested account functions:

  • Primary wallet identity: the public EVM wallet address and chain identifier selected for the account.
  • Account record: a stable internal account identifier, account creation timestamp, user-selected or generated public profile name where available, avatar selection and optional referral value.
  • Authentication data: short-lived challenge metadata, nonce/request identifiers, signature verification result, session expiry and a cryptographic hash of the session token. The raw reusable wallet secret and private key are not stored.
  • Consent records: the accepted Terms of Use and Privacy Policy version, acceptance timestamp, wallet-loss warning version and acknowledgement timestamp.
  • Genesis programme data: the profile linked to the same AstroCrown account ID, public display name, programme status, linked public network addresses, ownership-proof metadata, programme entitlement status and relevant public payment or purchase evidence if those features are later enabled.
  • Security and abuse-prevention data: request timestamps, rate-limit counters and keyed digests used to apply request limits. Infrastructure providers may independently process network and diagnostic metadata as described in their own notices.

The precise fields and retention periods must be kept aligned with the deployed schema and production infrastructure before this draft is approved.

4. Information not requested for wallet authentication

The ordinary wallet account sign-in flow does not require an email address, phone number, password or passkey credential. AstroCrown does not request or store the wallet's private key, seed phrase, mnemonic or recovery words. No ordinary-account passkey login, password reset, email/SMS recovery or one-time passkey recovery key is provided.

A wallet public address is pseudonymous, not anonymous. It may be associated with public blockchain transactions and activity by anyone using public network data. If you use the same address across services or publish it elsewhere, other people may be able to associate those activities.

5. What the wallet signature does

The sign-in message is an off-chain personal-message proof of control of the selected public address. The signature is verified by the AstroCrown Worker to establish the account session. It is not broadcast to a blockchain, does not require gas and does not authorize a transaction, token approval, transfer, payment or spending permission.

Connecting a wallet provider and exposing an address is distinct from signing the authentication challenge. The user chooses the provider and confirms the address. AstroCrown does not silently select an address from an account list when multiple addresses are available.

6. Wallet loss and account recovery

The primary wallet is the only ordinary AstroCrown account recovery anchor. To regain access, you must restore the same wallet and address through your wallet provider's own recovery process or an offline backup you created.

If you permanently lose access to the primary wallet and every restoration path, access to the associated AstroCrown account is permanently lost. AstroCrown cannot reset or recreate the wallet, recover its secret words, bypass wallet proof or move the account to another address. AstroCrown staff and administrators will never ask you for seed phrases, private keys or recovery words.

7. Genesis and additional network addresses

Genesis programme data is stored in programme-specific records but is bound to the same AstroCrown account ID. This separation keeps programme, entitlement and financial-evidence records distinct from ordinary account session data; it does not create a second account or authentication authority.

The primary identity wallet is automatically associated with the Genesis profile as its primary Ethereum address. Additional Ethereum, TRON or Solana addresses may be linked to the same account for specific supported programme functions. Each additional address must independently prove control using a fresh off-chain message signature. A linked address is not an alternate login or recovery identity.

In the current staging environment, Genesis payment acceptance, qualifying payments, purchases and automatic token issuance are disabled. No funds should be sent. If payment functionality is approved in the future, the policy must be updated to identify the payment data collected, the actual processing purposes, providers, retention and legal basis before the feature is enabled.

8. Payments and automated permissions

Sign-in, wallet connection and additional-address linking do not authorize spending. When payments are eventually enabled, every payment must display the asset, exact amount, network, destination and applicable fees and require a separate confirmation in the user's wallet. Ordinary account sign-in must never create an unlimited token approval or standing spending permission.

Any future automated trading or payment tool must require a separate, explicit, narrowly scoped, capped, time-limited and revocable authorization. No such automated spending authorization is active in staging.

9. Service providers and transfers

The staging service is hosted through Cloudflare Workers and D1. A user's wallet provider is an independent service selected by the user. AstroCrown does not control the wallet provider's software, account recovery, security procedures or independent data practices. Cloudflare and other providers may process technical information needed to deliver, secure and operate their services.

The production version of this policy must identify the actual service providers, relevant processing roles, data locations and any international transfers, and must link to the providers' current privacy notices. This development draft is not a complete provider or transfer register.

10. Retention, deletion and account closure

Staging data is temporary and may be reset or deleted as part of qualification or cleanup. Session credentials expire and session-token hashes are retained only as needed for the account session and security controls. Challenge records are intended to be short-lived and single-use. Programme and public-ledger evidence may need different retention treatment from disposable account sessions because it can support audit, entitlement or public verification functions.

The final retention schedule, deletion procedure, backup lifecycle, handling of immutable public blockchain information and process for privacy requests must be reviewed and published before production. Closing an AstroCrown account cannot erase information already recorded on a public blockchain by a user or third party.

11. Communications, feedback and case access

Email, SMS, newsletter delivery, private account messages, feedback and grievance/case access are not enabled in the current staging qualification. This staging account does not subscribe you to a mailing list and does not send email or SMS messages.

If these features are introduced, AstroCrown must publish their specific purposes, data fields, access controls, retention, delivery providers and available choices before collecting real information for them.

12. Security

AstroCrown uses server-side signature verification, short-lived challenges, account sessions, origin checks and rate limits to protect the wallet account flow. No security measure can guarantee prevention of every incident. The production security description must be kept consistent with the actual deployed implementation and reviewed after material changes.

13. Your privacy choices and requests

Applicable privacy laws may provide rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or other handling of personal information. The process, exceptions, identity-verification requirements, response timelines and contact details must be specified for the production operator and relevant jurisdictions before real users are onboarded.

14. Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy should identify the new version and effective date and be communicated where reasonably practicable. Renewed acknowledgement or consent will be requested where required by law or appropriate to the change.

Before production launch, AstroCrown must publish the identity and contact details of the responsible service operator and a working privacy-request contact. Those details have not yet been approved in this development draft.
