AstroCrown · Account access

Wallet identity & transaction safety

AstroCrown uses one primary wallet as the identity and recovery anchor for each ordinary user account. This page explains what that means, what the wallet signature does, how account recovery works and the safeguards required before payment or automation features are enabled.

Current staging status: wallet sign-in only. Payments and automated spending are disabled.

The staging environment is public and temporary. Use a disposable wallet with no valuable assets and invented account details. Do not use a production wallet or real funds.

One AstroCrown account, one primary identity wallet

  • The primary wallet address is the account identity. It is not an email address, password or separate Genesis account.
  • AstroCrown offers wallet-provider selection and, where a provider exposes several addresses, asks you to choose the address for your account. The browser may ask you to approve access to that public address.
  • Genesis membership, public profile, badge and programme entitlements belong to this same AstroCrown account. Genesis does not create a second login or independent recovery system.
  • Additional Ethereum, TRON or Solana addresses may be linked later for a specific supported programme function. Each additional address must prove control with its own fresh, off-chain message signature. It does not become another account identity.

What signing in asks your wallet to do

  • AstroCrown requests the public address and network identifier from the wallet provider, then issues a short-lived, single-use challenge bound to this website origin and the selected address.
  • The wallet signs a readable EIP-191 personal message. The server verifies that the signature belongs to the selected externally owned EVM account before creating a session.
  • This is an off-chain message signature. It is not broadcast to a blockchain, does not require gas, and does not authorize a transaction, token approval, transfer, payment or spending permission.
  • During account creation, AstroCrown separately records your acceptance of the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy and your acknowledgement of the wallet-loss warning. The server checks both acknowledgements; the browser checkbox alone is not treated as proof.

AstroCrown never receives or stores your wallet private key, seed phrase, mnemonic or recovery words. Never enter or send them to AstroCrown, its staff, support personnel, NOVA or anyone else.

Wallet loss and account recovery

Your primary wallet is your AstroCrown identity. To recover access, use the wallet provider’s own recovery process or an offline backup you created to restore the same wallet and address.

If you permanently lose access to that wallet and every way of restoring it, access to the associated AstroCrown account is permanently lost. AstroCrown staff and administrators cannot reset the wallet, recreate its secret words, bypass wallet proof or move the account to a different address.

Payment confirmation: separate from sign-in

Wallet sign-in is not permission to spend. When payment functionality is eventually enabled, each payment must be presented for a separate review and confirmation in the wallet. The confirmation must clearly identify:

  • The asset or token and its contract or mint identity.
  • The exact amount and any network or service fees.
  • The blockchain network and the destination address or contract.
  • The action being authorized, including any token allowance or contract interaction.

Normal sign-in and address linking must never grant unlimited token approval or standing payment authority. Where a payment requires an allowance, the default design is a separate, explicit approval limited to the amount and purpose that the user has reviewed.

Future automated trading or payment tools

An automated tool or trading bot must not inherit spending authority from ordinary account sign-in. Any future delegation must be a separate, optional authorization with clear controls, including:

  • Explicit opt-in and a plain-language explanation of what the tool can do.
  • Allowed assets, networks, destinations and contract functions.
  • Per-transaction and cumulative spending limits, with no unlimited allowance.
  • A short, visible expiry, a simple revocation mechanism and an audit trail.
  • Clear notifications and a way to review or stop the authorization.

No automated spending authorization is active in AstroCrown staging.

Privacy and implementation limits

  • A public wallet address is pseudonymous, not anonymous. It may be linked to public blockchain activity by anyone.
  • The current primary-account sign-in path supports EVM externally owned accounts. Contract-wallet signature verification and non-EVM primary account sign-in are not qualified by this release.
  • Additional network addresses for Genesis purposes require their own ownership proof and remain subordinate to the same AstroCrown primary account.
  • Staging is not the production commercial service and is not a guarantee of continuous availability or permanent test-account retention.
