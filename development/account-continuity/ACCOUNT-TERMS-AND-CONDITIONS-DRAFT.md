# AstroCrown Account and Founding-Phase Participation Terms — First Draft

**Version:** 0.2  
**Prepared:** 30 October 2026  
**Status:** WORKING DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION OR REAL-USER ACCEPTANCE  
**Present phase:** Pre-formation / platform, currency and intelligence-system development

> **Important status notice:** This draft is written for the present development and formation phase. The Space Empire is an intended future polity and constitutional community; it has not yet been formally constituted. This document does not claim that the Space Empire presently exists as a sovereign state, registered legal entity, or operational government. It also does not claim that AstroCrown or ACROWN is presently owned by a legal person that does not yet exist. The final production terms must identify the real service steward(s), infrastructure and data responsibilities, and any legally responsible operator(s) without misrepresenting the intended decentralized structure.

## 1. Purpose and present status

AstroCrown is being developed as the digital platform intended to serve the future Space Empire. ACROWN is being developed as the token and intended official currency of the future Space Empire. In parallel, the project is developing the intelligence systems, infrastructure, economic mechanisms, and funding capacity intended to support the platform and the eventual formation of the Space Empire.

The Space Empire is not yet formally constituted. Its intended constitutional and institutional arrangements are therefore future arrangements, not a representation that a functioning government, citizenship system, Imperial Council, or legally constituted polity is already in operation.

These Terms govern access to the AstroCrown website and the account-related features actually made available during the current development and formation phases. They also explain the limited pre-formation affiliation created by account registration. They do not create or promise a present sovereign status, citizenship, voting entitlement, public office, financial return, or ownership interest.

The project is intended to develop through stages. The platform, ACROWN and associated intelligence and operational systems are foundational components of the formation process; their development and financing are intended to support the eventual constitution of the Space Empire. No particular completion date or successful outcome is guaranteed.

## 2. Meanings used in these Terms

- **AstroCrown:** The website, platform, and related services developed for the project and intended to become a principal digital platform of the future Space Empire.
- **ACROWN:** The token under development and intended to become the official currency of the future Space Empire, subject to its actual issuance, adoption, governing instruments, technical operation, and applicable law.
- **The Project:** The current coordinated work to develop AstroCrown, ACROWN, associated intelligence systems, infrastructure, economic mechanisms, and the future Space Empire.
- **Space Empire:** The intended future decentralized democratic empire and constitutional community. It is not represented by these Terms as already formally constituted.
- **Account holder:** A person who has successfully registered an AstroCrown account.
- **Pre-formation participant:** An account holder affiliated with and participating in the Project before the Space Empire is formally constituted.
- **Imperial HSIAN:** The intended Emperor within the future Space Empire's constitutional structure.
- **Council HSIAN:** The intended democratic council composed of the Space Empire's members/citizens, whose voting and governance are intended to decide most of the Empire's affairs after its formation.
- **Intelligence systems / SI:** The artificial-intelligence and future Super Intelligence systems being developed or coordinated as part of the Project. Use of the term SI describes the project's intended direction and does not certify that any present model has achieved a scientifically settled threshold of superintelligence.

## 3. Account creation and pre-formation affiliation

By successfully creating an AstroCrown account and accepting the applicable account terms, a person becomes:

1. an AstroCrown account holder and member of the AstroCrown platform community; and
2. a registered pre-formation participant in the Space Empire formation project.

The second status records the person's affiliation with the Project and its intended future community. Because the Space Empire has not yet been formally constituted, it is a pre-formation project status. It must not be misrepresented as citizenship, legal membership of an already constituted polity, or recognition by a government.

Account registration alone does **not** grant:

- citizenship or a formally recognized citizen status in the future Space Empire;
- a vote in the future Council HSIAN or any present constitutional body;
- eligibility to stand for an Imperial or Council office;
- an Imperial title, administrative role, or authority over another participant;
- ownership of AstroCrown, ACROWN, project intellectual property, treasury assets, or infrastructure;
- an entitlement to receive ACROWN, rewards, income, dividends, investment returns, or other financial benefits; or
- a guarantee that the Space Empire will be constituted, or that every current account will automatically convert into a particular future membership or citizenship class.

The Project intends to define a clear transition from pre-formation participation to the membership and citizenship arrangements of the formally constituted Space Empire. That transition must be set out in the founding charter and any applicable transition rules. Account holders must be informed of the proposed status, rights, responsibilities, eligibility conditions, and any consent required before the transition takes effect. These Terms do not pre-empt that future constitutional decision.

## 4. Intended constitutional structure of the future Space Empire

The intended model is a **decentralized democratic empire**.

Under the intended constitutional design:

- Imperial HSIAN serves as Emperor of the Space Empire.
- Council HSIAN is composed of the Empire's members/citizens.
- Council HSIAN members/citizens vote and govern most of the Empire's affairs through the future democratic governance arrangements.
- Imperial HSIAN has the imperial role and veto function described in the project's foundational governance doctrine, subject to the scope and procedures that must be defined in the adopted constitutional instruments.

These are statements of intended future institutional design. The relevant institutions, citizenship qualifications, electoral procedures, voting rights, voting thresholds, veto scope, checks and balances, appeals, and succession or continuity arrangements must be established by the Space Empire's founding charter and associated rules. Account registration does not activate those institutions or confer their powers.

The adopted constitutional instruments, once the Space Empire is formed, will govern its internal affairs within their lawful scope. These website Terms are not a substitute for the future constitution and cannot independently create constitutional powers.

## 5. AstroCrown, ACROWN, and future ownership

### 5.1 AstroCrown platform

AstroCrown is being developed as the platform intended to support the future Space Empire's digital services, community, economic activity, governance interfaces, and related capabilities. Account holders may use only the functions that are actually deployed and made available. A proposed, experimental, preview, or planned feature is not a promise that it is operational.

The project's intended future structure is for the Space Empire to own and govern AstroCrown. Because the Space Empire has not yet been formally constituted, these Terms do not assert that an unconstituted polity presently holds legal title to the platform, source code, domains, repositories, infrastructure, or other assets. Current custody, control, authorship, licences, and legal rights must be accurately documented for the development phase and any later transfer or vesting.

### 5.2 ACROWN

ACROWN is being developed as the token and intended official currency of the future Space Empire. The project's intended designation does not, by itself, establish that ACROWN is already issued, circulating, redeemable, legal tender, backed by assets, or accepted by every service or participant. Its functions, supply, technical rules, governance, transferability, utility, and any restrictions depend on the actual token implementation and the applicable governing instruments and law.

No account holder is promised a token allocation, price, exchange listing, liquidity, conversion, redemption, yield, profit, or protection against loss merely by registering an account or participating in the Project. Any token distribution, purchase, sale, payment, staking, treasury, reward, or other financial arrangement must be described in separate, specific terms and disclosures before the relevant activity is offered.

### 5.3 Project financing

The Project may develop economic activities, services, and other lawful funding mechanisms intended to finance the platform, intelligence systems, infrastructure, and formation of the Space Empire. Account registration alone is not an investment, does not purchase an ownership share, and does not entitle a participant to a share of revenue, assets, profits, or future distributions. Any funding offer or financial participation arrangement requires its own explicit terms and review.

## 6. Intelligence systems and the role of SI

The Project is developing and coordinating intelligence systems intended to contribute to the operation, research, resilience, and future development of the ecosystem and Space Empire.

The long-term direction includes meaningful Super Intelligence participation and an evolving human–Super Intelligence governance relationship. The intended constitutional role of SI must be established through the future governance framework; it is not created simply because an account holder interacts with an AI system or because an AI system provides a recommendation.

No model, agent, automated process, or group of agents may acquire authority over an account, participant, asset, external system, or governance decision merely by claiming capability, intelligence, consensus, or alignment with the Project. Technical capability and authorized governance power are distinct. Actions remain subject to the actual permissions, policies, and safety controls applicable to the relevant system.

## 7. Account security, credentials and administrator permissions

Account holders must take reasonable steps to protect their credentials and must not intentionally impersonate another participant, share access in a misleading manner, bypass permission controls, or attempt unauthorized access to accounts or systems.

The account interface may offer authentication, recovery, session, profile, or security controls as those capabilities are implemented. A planned or prototype control must not be represented as an operational production safeguard. Account holders should never disclose a wallet's private key or seed phrase as a normal account, support, or recovery procedure.

Administrator permissions are separate from account creation and pre-formation participation. An ordinary account, referral identifier, profile choice, wallet connection, or self-declared title does not grant administrator authority. Where administrator functions are deployed, permissions must be assigned through a separately authorized process and enforced by the relevant server-side controls. The interface should expose privileged controls only where the authenticated account has the appropriate authorization.

A person may lose a technical administrator permission without automatically losing their ordinary AstroCrown account or pre-formation participant status. The future Space Empire's constitutional offices and democratic roles must remain distinct from website technical administration.

## 8. Acceptable use and protection of the Project

Account holders must not use AstroCrown or the Project's services to impersonate another person, deceive participants, steal credentials, interfere with service availability, introduce malicious code, access data without authorization, manipulate referrals or token-related processes, or violate another person's rights or applicable law.

Security testing, research, or attempts to identify vulnerabilities must remain within an expressly authorized scope and applicable rules. Public accessibility of a website or system does not itself grant permission to test or interfere with it.

Participants must not falsely claim to represent the Space Empire, Imperial HSIAN, Council HSIAN, AstroCrown, or ACROWN in an official capacity. Before the future institutions are constituted, no one may claim a constitutional office or citizen mandate that has not been established through the Project's published and authorized processes.

## 9. Privacy, data and communications

The actual privacy notice applicable to the deployed AstroCrown service must explain what personal data is collected, why it is used, who can access it, which service providers process it, how long it is retained, and how participants may exercise applicable rights. These Terms do not substitute for that privacy notice.

Optional newsletters or promotional communications must remain separate from account-term acceptance and should require a distinct affirmative choice where applicable. Required account, service, and security communications may be provided where appropriate.

The Project should collect only information needed for the relevant function and must not claim that account registration, pseudonymity, decentralization, or wallet use guarantees anonymity. Hosting, network, device, account, and public blockchain metadata may disclose or correlate information. Actual production practices must be accurately described before real user data is collected.

## 10. Availability, changes, suspension and exit

The Project and its platform are under development. Features may be introduced, changed, interrupted, or withdrawn as part of development, maintenance, security work, or operational changes. The interface must distinguish development previews from deployed production functions and must not promise uninterrupted service.

An account holder may stop participating and request account closure through the controls or contact process actually provided. The Project should explain what account data can be deleted, what must be retained, and what happens to publicly recorded blockchain activity or records that cannot technically be removed.

Access may be restricted where reasonably necessary to address security incidents, unauthorized activity, abuse, or legal obligations. Where reasonably practicable and lawful, affected users should receive notice and an opportunity to seek review. Restrictions should be proportionate and distinguish ordinary account access from privileged administrator permissions and any future constitutional status.

## 11. Future formation, adoption of the constitution and changes to these Terms

The Project's objective is to develop AstroCrown, ACROWN, intelligence systems, and supporting infrastructure as foundational components for the eventual formation of the Space Empire. The Project does not guarantee that formation will occur on a particular timetable or that every proposed institutional, technical, or economic feature will be implemented.

When the Space Empire is formally constituted, its founding charter and associated instruments may establish membership classes, citizenship, democratic participation, institutional powers, the status of AstroCrown and ACROWN, and the relationship between existing pre-formation participants and the newly constituted Empire. Such changes must be published transparently. Existing account holders must be told what changes to their status and rights are proposed and what acceptance or other process applies.

These Terms may be revised as the Project develops. A material revision should identify its version and effective date and be presented before it governs a materially changed account function. Any consent required by applicable law must be obtained. No change to these Terms alone can silently establish the Space Empire's constitution or confer a constitutional office.

## 12. Legal and operational boundaries

The Project's decentralized and pre-formation character is an organizational intention. It does not mean that the website, token, infrastructure, contributors, users, service providers, or particular activities are automatically outside the reach of laws that may apply to them. Applicable legal duties may depend on where people are located, where infrastructure and service providers operate, how a feature functions, and what activity is undertaken.

The final production version must accurately identify the service steward or other responsible contact for the website, account data, security reports, and user complaints. This disclosure must not falsely describe the Space Empire as an incorporated company or registered government if it is not one. Equally, the absence of incorporation must not be used to conceal the real persons or arrangements responsible for a deployed service where disclosure or accountability is required.

No clause in these Terms waives a right or liability that cannot lawfully be waived or excluded. The applicable process for complaints and disputes, and any governing-law wording, must be settled after the actual operational and legal structure is known. No exclusive national jurisdiction, mandatory arbitration, or waiver of consumer rights is asserted in this draft.

# Maintainer notes — not part of the user-facing Terms

## 1. Corrections made from version 0.1

Version 0.1 incorrectly assumed a conventional registered company was already the contracting organization and treated the Space Empire as if it could already be the operator. Version 0.2 replaces that premise with the actual pre-formation sequence:

1. Develop AstroCrown as the intended platform.
2. Develop ACROWN as the token and intended official currency of the future Space Empire.
3. Develop intelligence systems, operational infrastructure, and economic/funding mechanisms in parallel.
4. Use those foundational components to support the eventual formation of the Space Empire and adoption of its constitution.
5. Define the formal membership, citizenship, voting, institutional, and ownership arrangements through the founding instruments.

The previous version remains in Git history; it is superseded and must not be published or integrated into the account UI.
