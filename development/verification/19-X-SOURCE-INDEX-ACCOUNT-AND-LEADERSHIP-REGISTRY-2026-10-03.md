# X Source-Index Account and Leadership Registry — 2026-10-03

## Purpose

The External Source Index is a distinct verification input set. Every source listed there is therefore checked separately for:
1. current organizational X account;
2. relevant current leadership/executive account where meaningful;
3. account-state changes, suspension, replacement or uncertainty.

“CEO” is not used where the entity is a standard, regulator, standards body, government agency, research service, or other organization with a different leadership structure.

## Status vocabulary

- VERIFIED — authoritative/current first-party material or a strong organization-controlled source identifies the account.
- OBSERVED — current account/identity is strongly matched, but first-party linkage is incomplete.
- HISTORICAL / REPLACED — former account or changed destination.
- SUSPENDED — account currently shows as suspended; do not use it as a current communication endpoint without re-verification.
- NOT VERIFIED — no sufficiently reliable account established in this pass.
- N/A — a single organizational X identity is not meaningful for the item.

## Why the leadership layer matters

Organizational accounts and executive accounts are different evidence objects. A corporate X account can be the correct operational communication channel while a CEO/founder account can be useful for leadership-level visibility, conference interaction, ecosystem relationships or public announcements. Neither should be treated as equivalent to the organization's legal, regulatory, security, listing, accounting or contractual authority.

The registry therefore records:
**organization account → relevant leadership role → personal/public account → verification source → effective date → account state**

This prevents stale CEO handles from surviving leadership transitions and avoids mistaking a prominent individual's account for an official institutional channel.

## Contact-security rule

For any consequential contact:

official website / current official source
→ current organizational account
→ current leadership account only when appropriate
→ identity cross-check
→ purpose-specific channel
→ non-sensitive public communication

X is never the sole authority for a regulatory, legal, audit, security, accounting, listing or contractual conclusion.

Never send private keys, seed phrases, wallet credentials, API secrets, financial credentials or similarly sensitive material through X.
