# K. Competitor Verification Benchmark

## Bitcoin / Bitcoin Core
Observed layers: open-source review, issue tracking, responsible disclosure, security advisories and CVE-linked public disclosures.
Derived AstroCrown lesson: public engineering history and reproducible disclosure create durable trust evidence but do not automatically replace independent assurance for new high-value systems.

## Ethereum
Observed layer: standing public bug bounty covering protocol, clients, specifications, network and cryptographic issues.
Derived lesson: vulnerability intake is an operating capability, not only a pre-launch event.

## Aave
Observed layers: independent audits/reviews, formal verification, fuzzing/invariant testing, public contests, standing bounty, audit archive, governance controls and public SOC 2 Type II attestation.
Derived lesson: layered assurance exposes different failure modes rather than compressing everything into an audited/not-audited label.

## Uniswap
Observed layers: multiple independent audits/reviews, historical formal verification and continuing bounty mechanisms.
Derived lesson: audit provenance should be tied to version/release scope rather than treated as permanent system-wide proof.

## Circle / reserve-backed systems
Observed layer: recurring reserve disclosure plus recurring third-party assurance.
Derived lesson: where a reserve-backed ACROWN architecture becomes applicable, recurring reconciliation/assurance is more informative than a one-time statement.

## Solana / Chainlink / Sky-Maker
Current evidence set was insufficient to populate every requested field without speculation. Keep detailed records as CANDIDATE / INVESTIGATION BACKLOG.

## Causal-impact caution
The evidence does not establish a defensible causal ranking of which verification layers produced the “greatest” trust or visibility impact. Record observed mechanisms and evidence instead.
