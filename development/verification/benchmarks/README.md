# Verification Benchmarks

This directory contains benchmark-specific methods, governance, pilot plans and artifacts when they serve a distinct project need. Do not duplicate an external benchmark merely to reproduce it; use it when it adequately answers the requirement.

## AstroCrown Security Assessment Service and Benchmark

- [00 — Security Assessment and Benchmark Starting Point](00-STARTING-POINT.md) — concise feature summary, candidate plans/pricing, fairness rules, launch sequence and unresolved decisions.

- [Security Assessment Service and Benchmark Governance Charter](ASTROCROWN-SECURITY-ASSESSMENT-AND-BENCHMARK-CHARTER.md) — proposed service scope, private assessment reports, optional public awards, Council conflict-of-interest rules and candidate pricing.

**Status: PROPOSED / NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLIC LAUNCH.** The charter does not prove that the scanner, plans, ranking, awards or report-delivery service are operational. Proposed prices require cost and capability validation. Do not change homepage claims until launch gates are passed and promotion is separately approved.

## External competitor research

The [Competitor Verification Benchmark](../11-COMPETITOR-VERIFICATION-BENCHMARK.md) records research about external projects. It is not the customer-facing AstroCrown Security Benchmark described by the charter.

## Artifact rule

Keep proposed methods separate from measured pilot results. Future benchmark artifacts should record scope, method version, authorization, test date, evidence, limitations, status and review decision. Preserve historical edition results rather than rewriting them.
