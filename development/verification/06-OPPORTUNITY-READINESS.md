# F. Opportunity Readiness Registry

The ecosystem must not be modeled as a fixed Development → Launch → Growth → Institutional ladder. Opportunities can appear unexpectedly. Readiness is modeled by trigger and required evidence.

| Opportunity | Earliest practical stage | Typical stage | Latest useful stage | Stage independent? | Evidence to keep ready | Preparation gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| External smart-contract audit | Before material production value | Pre-mainnet / major upgrade | Post-deployment re-audit | Sometimes | Frozen commit, architecture, scope, tests, threat model | audit package + assessor shortlist |
| Formal verification | Once properties are specified | Pre-production / critical upgrade | Post-deployment maintenance | Yes | invariants, specs, source/bytecode mapping | property catalog |
| Bug bounty | Once attack surface is meaningful | Live/public system | Continuously | Yes | scope, exclusions, severity, response policy | policy + funding/disclosure workflow |
| Exchange listing | Once listing-ready asset exists | Whenever opportunity appears | Post-listing for additional venues | Yes | legal/compliance, tokenomics, contracts, security, market/community data | master exchange dossier |
| Institutional due diligence | Before active institutional engagement | Opportunity-driven | Renewal/expansion | Yes | finance, governance, security, treasury, legal evidence | evidence room |
| Major media | Before outreach | Opportunistic | Continuously | Yes | verified fact pack, source pack, disclosures | media dossier |
| Academic publication | Once research artifact exists | When research is mature | Follow-up after publication | Yes | data, methods, code, provenance, conflicts | reproducible research package |
| Regulatory authorization | Before regulated activity | Entry into regulated perimeter | Continuous | Usually | entity structure, AML/KYC, disclosures, governance, incident controls | jurisdictional perimeter map |
| Reserve attestation | When reserve-backed design exists | Initial issuance + recurring reporting | Continuous | Yes | reserve policy, custody, reconciliations, circulation data | assurance architecture |
| Incident response | From first public attack surface | Continuous | Always | Yes | contacts, logs, runbooks, disclosure policy | incident-response package |

## Readiness principle

The objective is to make important opportunities actionable without pretending that every pathway should be activated immediately.
