## Boundary with development/audit/

development/audit/ remains the record of an actual audit/verification activity: scope, criteria, method, evidence, findings, remediation, re-test and closure. This subsystem is the intelligence layer that discovers and prepares the ecosystem.

## Evidence rules

Preserve provenance, conflicts, uncertainty and version/date scope. Never turn UNKNOWN into FALSE, CANDIDATE into FACT, or BLOCKED into PASS. Never present an internal NOVA simulation as an external certification, audit, regulator decision, exchange decision, accounting attestation, or independent editorial decision.
