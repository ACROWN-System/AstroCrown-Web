# 18. Important interpretation

The highest-value future target is often **the person one level below the public figure**.

For example:

- a CEO may create visibility, but a partner may control an investment thesis;
- a fund's public account may announce a deal, but a principal may source and diligence it;
- an exchange CEO may be highly visible, but the listing/asset-review team handles the actual process;
- a media CEO rarely chooses individual stories; an editor or reporter usually does;
- a large influencer may attract attention, but a producer/manager may choose guests and commercial collaborations;
- a protocol founder may amplify AstroCrown, while the ecosystem/BD team determines technical integration;
- a regulator's public account may disseminate information, while formal regulatory channels control authoritative outcomes.

Future research should therefore build **relationship graphs**, not only follower lists.

# 19. Preservation rule

Do not delete old targets because an account or role becomes inactive.

**current → replaced → historical → archived**

- account suspension;

- organization closure;
